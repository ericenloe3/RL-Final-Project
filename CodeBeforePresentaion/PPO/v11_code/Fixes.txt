Observation size is still 43, so the train/eval scripts don't need updates. The reward change is:
Old (position-based proximity): R_PROXIMITY × (1 - (dist - capture_dist) / (zone - capture_dist)). This gave a constant bonus every step the hunter was within 60px, regardless of whether distance was increasing, decreasing, or oscillating. At dist=40, the hunter earned +0.075/step just for existing near the prey — even while bouncing back and forth.
New (closing-velocity proximity): R_CLOSING × max(0, Δdist) / speed. This gives a bonus only when distance actually decreases within the proximity zone. Oscillating (Δdist alternates ±2px) averages to ~0 bonus. Steadily closing (Δdist = +4.5/step) earns +0.15/step. The hunter's optimal strategy shifts from "be close" to "get closer," which naturally resolves the oscillation — the only way to maximize the closing bonus is to actually close the gap and capture.
The prey penalty mirrors this: it's penalized only when distance decreases against it, not just for being near the hunter. So the prey's incentive shifts from "don't be close" to "don't let the gap close" — which is satisfied by actively fleeing rather than by standing still at 40px.
