Bug 1 — Stochastic sampling at eval time (models.py).  get_action() was always calling Normal(mean, std).sample(). Even a perfectly trained policy has non-zero std — that's intentional during training to encourage exploration. But at evaluation time this adds random Gaussian noise to every action. Visually this looks like the agent occasionally darts in the wrong direction for no reason, or oscillates. The fix adds a deterministic flag that returns mean directly, bypassing sampling entirely. evaluate.py now passes deterministic=True.
Bug 2 — Hardcoded CAPTURE_DIST = 30 in evaluate.py. This duplicated AGENT_SIZE * 2 from the env with no link between them. If the env constant changed, captures would be silently misreported. The fix imports CAPTURE_DIST directly from my_game_env.
To be clear on what these fixes do and don't fix: the loading was always correct. These fixes make the evaluation faithfully show what the policy actually learned. If agents still look random after retraining with the new env, that means training needs more time — but at least now you'll be seeing the true policy behavior rather than the true policy plus noise.
