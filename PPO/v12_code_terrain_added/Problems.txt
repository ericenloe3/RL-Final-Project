Three root causes found
1. Prey log_std exploded — the prey never actually trained (train.py)
The training log revealed prey entropy growing linearly from 3.0 → 86.6 over all 15M steps, implying log_std ≈ 42 (std ≈ 10¹⁸). The log_std parameter had no bounds, and with 8 PPO epochs per batch pushing entropy upward at ENT_COEF=0.05, it compounded unboundedly. At log_std=42, sampled actions are pure Gaussian noise at astronomical scale — after tanh, they're effectively random ±1 regardless of what the mean network outputs. The policy gradient averages to near-zero over random actions, so the prey learned essentially nothing after the first few hundred thousand steps.
Fix: Added _clamped_log_std property to ActorCritic that clamps to [-3, 0.5] (std range 0.05–1.65). This is used everywhere log_std was previously accessed directly. Also reduced prey ENT_COEF end from 0.015 → 0.005, and added log_std to the training log so you can confirm it stays bounded.
2. Hunter wall-obstacle corner trap — no stuck signal in observation (env.py)
Ep4 gif shows the hunter at (639, 73) oscillating for 400 steps, avg speed 0.23 px/step vs rated 4.5. It was wedged against an obstacle face near the top-right wall — the policy kept outputting "east toward prey" but the collision system silently returned near-zero displacement. The policy had no way to know its action failed; there was no difference in observation between "I just moved 4.5px" and "I just moved 0.2px."
Fix: Two new observation features per agent — own_disp_frac (actual last-step displacement / rated speed, 0=stuck, 1=free) and own_last_bumped (binary flag). The hunter can now learn: "disp_frac=0.05 + bumped=1 → try a different direction." obs_size increases by 2 (67 total).
3. Terrain curriculum too late + no prey incentive (env.py + train.py)
Mud was introduced at step 9M/15M (capture rate already 99%), ice at 12M — only 3M steps of ice training. With a 99% capture rate, the prey almost never survives long enough to experiment with terrain, so the gradient for ice-baiting is near-zero throughout.
