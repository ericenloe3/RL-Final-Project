Both env.py and train.py changed. Here's the diagnosis and each fix:
The core problem: the prey never experienced a successful escape. With 97–100% capture rate from step 1M onward, the prey's value function learned V(every state) ≈ -97. Every action was equally bad, so the policy just maximized the tiny per-step heading reward (+0.1/step × ~170 steps = +17) before the inevitable -100 capture. "Run straight away from hunter" was the locally optimal strategy because the prey had never seen the +20 timeout bonus that comes from surviving 500 steps.
env.py — 5 changes
Reduced speed gap (5.0→4.7 hunter, 4.0 prey unchanged): The hunter now closes at 0.7 px/step instead of 1.0. To cross 500px it takes 714 steps — longer than the 500-step episode limit. This means a prey that puts obstacles in the way has a realistic chance of running out the clock. The prey needs to experience escapes to learn that survival pays +30.
Doubled survival rewards: R_STEP_P from 0.01 to 0.02 (+10 over 500 steps) and R_TIMEOUT_P from 10 to 20. Full survival now earns +30 (30% of the capture penalty magnitude). The previous +15 was too small — the prey's "run straight and die" strategy earned +17 from heading before the -100 capture, so survival (+15) wasn't even clearly better. Now +30 is nearly double the heading-to-death path (+17), giving a clear gradient toward survival.
Stronger wall/corner penalties: Wall penalty -0.08 (80% of heading, was 50%), corner penalty -0.08 additional, zone widened to 40px. In a corner the prey nets -0.11/step — strongly negative. At a wall while fleeing it nets +0.04/step — still slightly positive, preserving the gradient to slide along the wall toward open space.
Prey spawns near obstacles (70% of episodes): _spawn_near_obstacle() picks a random obstacle, random face, and places the prey just outside. This gives the prey immediate cover access at the start of 70% of episodes, so it has early experience with LOS-breaking situations instead of having to stumble onto obstacles by chance.
Hunter timeout penalty doubled (-10→-20): Symmetrically, the hunter is now more motivated to catch the prey quickly rather than wandering.
train.py — 3 changes
10M total steps (was 8M): More training time for the prey to discover cover strategies.
Per-agent entropy (prey 0.05→0.01, hunter 0.02→0.003): The prey gets 2.5× higher entropy than the hunter throughout training. The hunter has already converged on a good pursuit policy — it doesn't need to explore. The prey needs to try dodging, juking, and cover-seeking strategies, which requires sustained high exploration. By the end of training the prey's entropy (0.01) is still 3× the hunter's (0.003).
Shorter no-obstacle phase (15% instead of 25%): The prey encounters obstacles at step 1.5M instead of 2M, giving 55% of training (5.5M steps) in the full-difficulty phase with 3–6 obstacles. More time with cover available means more chances to discover LOS-breaking.
